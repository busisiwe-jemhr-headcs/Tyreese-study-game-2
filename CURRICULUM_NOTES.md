# CAPS alignment notes — Grade 5 Term 3

This project uses South African DBE CAPS as the curriculum backbone and the supplied school revision memo as the higher-priority school-specific source where it gives explicit assessed topics.

## Term 3 map used in the app

- Mathematics: number operations/mental mathematics, fractions, angles/turns, geometry including parallel/perpendicular lines, squares/rectangles and quadrilaterals. The supplied Maths memorandum explicitly includes fractions, angles, directions/turns, parallel/perpendicular lines, squares/rectangles and quadrilaterals.
- Geography: weather, measurement/observation, rainfall patterns, weather vs climate, South African climates, natural vegetation and savannah.
- History: ancient African society — Egypt; source-based reasoning and paragraph writing.
- Natural Sciences & Technology: energy and movement; stored energy in elastic/springs; wheels and axles; fuels/fire; simple circuits and electricity.
- English Home Language: novel listening/reading, prediction, central events, characters, book review, grammar/conventions including pronouns, adjectives, adverbs, conjunctions, tenses, similes, proverbs and idioms.
- isiZulu: reading/comprehension, vocabulary, sentence construction, language structures and writing. The exact school scope should take precedence where the teacher has supplied a more specific Term 3 revision memo.
- Coding & Robotics: recognising/interpreting patterns, completing patterns/data sets, reading code line-by-line, changing code to create a different effect, robots, robot types and components.

## Important

The app is a study companion, not an official DBE assessment instrument. Teacher-provided scope always wins if it differs from a general CAPS sequence.
